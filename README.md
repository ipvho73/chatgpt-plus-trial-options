Searching for a **ChatGPT Plus free trial** usually means one of three things:

- You want to test Plus before paying for a full subscription.
- You have seen a referral link or promotional offer and want to know whether it is legitimate.
- You need a way to subscribe without using an international credit card.

These are different situations, but search results often blend them together. A free trial invite from OpenAI is not the same thing as a discounted subscription from a third-party platform. BeWild, also branded as WildAI, belongs to the second category: it is a paid subscription service that helps users activate AI subscriptions through local payment methods. It is not an official ChatGPT Plus free-trial provider.

That distinction matters before you enter payment details or hand over any account credentials.

## Does ChatGPT Plus currently offer a free trial?

OpenAI does not advertise a permanent, open-to-everyone ChatGPT Plus free trial. Instead, it may provide limited promotional trials or referral invitations to selected eligible users.

According to OpenAI’s promotional subscription FAQ, eligible users may receive trial links through email or inside ChatGPT. The exact duration, eligible account type, number of available invitations, region restrictions, and expiration date can vary by campaign. Some promotions may last for a limited period such as one month or several months, but the offer shown on your account is the controlling detail.

In practical terms, you should check these places:

1. Your ChatGPT account for an in-product promotion.
2. Emails sent directly by OpenAI.
3. A referral link shared by an eligible ChatGPT user.
4. The checkout screen before accepting the offer.

A random coupon page promising a universal “100% free ChatGPT Plus” code is not proof that an official trial exists. ChatGPT promotions can be limited to new users, free-plan users, certain countries, or a specific campaign audience. Existing Plus or Pro subscribers may be excluded, even if they cancel their current plan.

### What happens after an official trial ends?

A free trial may convert into a paid subscription automatically if a valid payment method is required and you do not cancel before the promotional period ends. OpenAI states that users should review the redemption page and subscription settings for the exact renewal terms.

That means “free” does not always mean “no payment details” and certainly does not mean “no need to monitor the renewal date.” Before accepting a trial, check:

- Whether a card or other payment method is required.
- The exact date the trial ends.
- The price charged after the trial.
- Whether cancellation must happen before the end date.
- Whether the offer applies to your existing account.

If the trial offer does not appear in your ChatGPT account or official email, there is no reliable way to force it to appear. Creating multiple accounts, repeatedly entering random promo codes, or using a referral link outside its intended audience can cause redemption errors or invalidate the offer.

The listed benefits include higher usage limits, priority access during busy periods, faster responses, advanced reasoning options, voice conversations, image generation, file uploads and analysis, and Deep Research features where available. Availability and usage limits can change during product rollouts.

The official Help Center currently lists ChatGPT Plus at **$20 per month**, billed monthly. API usage is separate and is not included in the ChatGPT Plus subscription.

The Free plan is still useful for occasional questions, basic writing, and limited access to tools. The difference becomes more noticeable when you frequently use uploads, image creation, longer conversations, advanced reasoning, or research features.

A free trial makes sense when you are unsure whether those higher limits justify a recurring payment. A third-party paid subscription makes more sense when your problem is payment access rather than product uncertainty.

## How the BeWild ChatGPT subscription process works

BeWild’s help documentation describes a multi-step flow rather than a normal OpenAI checkout.

### 1. Select ChatGPT and the subscription duration

Open the subscription area, choose ChatGPT, select the duration, and then choose the plan tier. The documentation distinguishes between a one-month subscription and longer subscriptions. It also warns that long-term plans may involve monthly automatic continuation or prepaid account credit being used month by month.

Read the payment summary carefully. Confirm:

- The exact ChatGPT account email.
- The plan name.
- The number of months.
- The total amount.
- The currency shown at checkout.
- Whether the plan renews or ends after the selected period.

### 2. Sign in to the ChatGPT account you want to upgrade

The process is intended to work with your existing ChatGPT account. BeWild’s guide instructs users to open ChatGPT in the same browser, sign in, and return to the BeWild page.

This is where account security deserves attention. A session JSON file or browser cookie can function as a sensitive authentication credential. It should be treated more seriously than an ordinary password-reset email.

### 3. Submit the requested account information

The guide instructs users to open the ChatGPT session endpoint while logged in and copy the resulting JSON into the BeWild form. For some higher-tier products, BeWild’s instructions describe an additional Cookie-Editor step.

Do not submit this information to an unofficial person, chat group, or unknown website. Use only the exact platform flow you have independently verified, and avoid reusing copied session data after the order is complete.

You should also avoid sharing:

- Your ChatGPT password.
- Two-factor authentication codes.
- Recovery codes.
- Personal payment card details outside the official checkout.
- Session information with anyone claiming to provide “manual support” in a private message.

### 4. Confirm the account and pay

The platform documentation says the page displays the detected account email before payment. Verify it carefully. If the wrong account is selected, the subscription may not be transferable.

The checkout may show a converted amount, such as a Hong Kong dollar amount, rather than the dollar amount shown on the product page. The final checkout total is the amount that matters.

### 5. Wait for processing

BeWild’s ChatGPT guide says processing can take around 5–15 minutes and advises users not to pay twice while the order is still processing.

A delayed order does not automatically mean that payment failed. Check the order status first. If the subscription fails, the platform states that failed subscriptions are refunded under its service policy, but you should read the applicable refund terms before purchasing.

## Official trial versus BeWild: which one should you choose?

The answer depends on the problem you are actually trying to solve.

### Choose an official OpenAI trial when:

- A valid trial invite is already visible in your ChatGPT account.
- You meet the eligibility conditions.
- You want to evaluate Plus without paying the initial subscription price.
- You are comfortable adding a payment method if the offer requires one.
- You can remember to cancel before automatic renewal if you do not want to continue.

An official trial is the cleaner route because the offer comes directly from OpenAI and does not require a third party to process your account subscription.

### Choose the official paid plan when:

- You can pay through OpenAI’s supported checkout.
- You want the simplest account relationship.
- You do not want to share session data or cookies with another platform.
- You expect to use Plus for several months.
- The official $20 monthly price is available to you.

OpenAI’s own sign-up path is straightforward: sign in to ChatGPT, open the account or profile menu, select the upgrade option, and choose Plus.

### Consider BeWild when:

- You cannot complete the official checkout with your available payment method.
- Local payment support is more important than paying directly through OpenAI.
- You understand that BeWild is a third-party service.
- You are willing to review its credential-handling, refund, and renewal instructions.
- You start with a shorter period before considering a multi-month plan.

The main convenience is payment access. The trade-off is that activation, troubleshooting, refunds, and subscription continuity involve an additional company.

## Important limitations before buying a third-party subscription

A third-party subscription does not remove the normal ChatGPT limitations. Plus users may still encounter message caps, tool-specific limits, regional restrictions, model rollouts, or temporary service issues. OpenAI says usage limits can vary depending on system conditions and product availability.

There is also a practical difference between buying directly from OpenAI and using a service that activates a subscription on your account:

- Direct OpenAI billing keeps payment, subscription management, and support in one place.
- Third-party activation adds another support layer.
- A failed order may require communication with the intermediary before a refund is processed.
- Multi-month arrangements may have special cancellation rules.
- Account credentials or session information may be part of the activation workflow.
- The exact product experience can change if OpenAI changes its subscription structure.

BeWild’s own documentation warns that users should not cancel the subscription from the official ChatGPT page during a multi-month arrangement because doing so may stop continuation and affect prepaid months.

That is a significant difference from a normal monthly subscription. Read the instructions for the exact plan before clicking “cancel.”

## Common ChatGPT Plus free-trial questions

### Can I use a ChatGPT Plus coupon to get a permanent free subscription?

No reliable public coupon can guarantee permanent free Plus access. Official trials are campaign-based, limited, and subject to eligibility rules. An ordinary discount code may reduce the price of a paid plan, but it does not necessarily create a free trial.

### Can existing ChatGPT users redeem a free trial?

Sometimes, but not always. OpenAI says eligibility depends on the specific campaign. Many referral offers focus on new users or people currently using the Free plan. Existing paid subscribers may be excluded.

### Do I need a payment method for a free trial?

Some promotions require one and some may not. OpenAI says the redemption flow will show whether a valid payment method is needed. If the trial converts automatically, cancel before the end of the promotional period to avoid the paid renewal.

### Is BeWild an official OpenAI partner?

The available BeWild landing page identifies WildAI as a service for subscribing to ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro, but it does not present BeWild as OpenAI itself. OpenAI’s official help and pricing pages should be treated as the source for official plans, features, and direct subscription terms.

### Is the BeWild one-month plan free?

No. The latest publicly indexed pricing snapshot lists a paid one-month ChatGPT Plus option. Its reference price was $22.99, subject to confirmation on the live checkout page.

### Should I start with one month or three months?

Start with one month if you are still evaluating the service, the activation process, or the account-handling requirements. A multi-month plan may reduce the monthly average, but it also commits more money before you know whether the service works well for your account.
